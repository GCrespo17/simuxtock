# US-001: Seed initial exchange state

## Story
**As an** Admin
**I want** the exchange to initialize tradable Assets, the Exchange Treasury, Market Bot inventories, and risk limits from startup seed configuration
**so that** the market opens with finite, conservation-safe balances and realistic starting prices without manual setup.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Startup seed configuration defines 5 target real-world Tickers, a local fallback list of 5 fictional Assets, Exchange Treasury cash, Market Bot inventories, and risk/rate limits.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Admin starts the exchange against an empty state.
- **BF-02:** System fetches company names and Initial Reference Prices for the 5 configured real-world Tickers from the external market data source.
- **BF-03:** System creates the 5 Assets (each with an empty isolated Order Book), seeds the Exchange Treasury account, seeds the Market Bot accounts with their configured cash and share inventories, and records the initial system totals.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-02): If the external market data source is unreachable or rate-limited, then System seeds the 5 Assets using the local fallback list of fictional Assets and their configured Initial Reference Prices. → Resume at BF-03.
- **AF-02** (from BF-01): If the exchange is already seeded (e.g., after a restart), then System skips seeding and preserves all existing Assets, Accounts, and prices without calling the external market data source. → End.

## Business rules
- **BR-01:** Initial seeding runs once on an empty exchange; post-seed prices move strictly from Trades executed on the exchange.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-02, BF-03, SRS/BR-11, SRS/BR-14): Given an empty exchange and an available external source returning `AAPL` at `$220.00` (and 4 other Tickers), when the exchange starts, then 5 Assets exist with their fetched Initial Reference Prices, the Exchange Treasury holds its configured cash, and all Market Bot accounts hold their configured finite cash and share inventories.
- **AC-02** (AF-01, BR-01): Given an empty exchange and an unavailable external source, when the exchange starts, then 5 fallback fictional Assets (e.g., `NOVA` at `$100.00`, `ACME` at `$45.00`) are created and the exchange boots normally.
- **AC-03** (AF-02, BR-01): Given an already-seeded exchange where `AAPL` has a Last Traded Price of `$225.50`, when the exchange restarts, then no external price fetch occurs and `AAPL` retains `$225.50`.

## Out of scope
- Runtime Asset creation, delisting, or Admin web UI.
