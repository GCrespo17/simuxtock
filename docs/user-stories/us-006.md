# US-006: Place a Limit Order

## Story
**As a** Trader
**I want** to place Limit Buy and Limit Sell orders at a chosen price and whole-share quantity
**so that** I can buy or sell at my target price or better and rest any unfilled portion on the Order Book.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Trader is signed in and selects a valid seeded Asset.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Trader submits a Limit Order specifying Asset, side (`BUY` or `SELL`), limit price, and whole-share quantity (plus an optional idempotency key).
- **BF-02:** System validates price tick (`$0.01` increments, `>= $0.01`), quantity (`1` to `10,000`), rate limits, active order cap (`50`), and Available balances, then reserves `qty × limit_price` (for Buy) or `qty` shares (for Sell).
- **BF-03:** System assigns the next per-Asset Sequence Number and matches the order against crossing opposite-side resting orders in Price-Time Priority (FIFO) at each Maker's price.
- **BF-04:** For each match, System creates an immutable Trade, settles cash and shares, and refunds any Price Improvement (`fill_qty × (buyer_limit_price - execution_price)`) to the Buyer's Available Cash.
- **BF-05:** If remaining quantity is `> 0`, System rests the remainder on the Order Book (`OPEN` or `PARTIALLY_FILLED`); otherwise marks the order `FILLED`.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-02): If price is `< $0.01`, not in `$0.01` increments, or quantity is `< 1`, fractional, or `> 10,000`, then System rejects the order without reserving balances. → End.
- **AF-02** (from BF-02): If the Trader lacks sufficient Available Cash (Buy) or Available Shares (Sell), then System marks the order `REJECTED` without modifying balances or the Order Book. → End.
- **AF-03** (from BF-03): If the incoming order would match a resting order owned by the same Account, then System auto-cancels the resting order (releasing its unfilled reservation) and continues matching against the next resting order. → Resume at BF-03.
- **AF-04** (from BF-02): If the Trader exceeds 10 order actions/sec (burst 20) or already has `50` active resting orders, then System rejects the order. → End.
- **AF-05** (from BF-01): If the Trader retries a request with a previously processed idempotency key, then System returns the original acknowledgment without placing a duplicate order. → End.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-02, BF-05, SRS/BR-10): Given `alice` has `$10,000.00` Available Cash and `AAPL` Best Ask is `$221.00`, when `alice` submits a Limit Buy for `10` `AAPL` at `$220.00`, then `$2,200.00` moves from Available Cash to Reserved Cash (`$7,800.00` Available) and the order rests as `OPEN` at `$220.00`.
- **AC-02** (BF-03, BF-04, SRS/BR-05, SRS/BR-06, SRS/BR-10): Given resting Asks at `$220.00` from `bob` (`5` shares, arrived first) and `carol` (`10` shares, arrived second), when `alice` submits a Limit Buy for `8` shares at `$221.00`, then two Trades execute at `$220.00` (`5` with `bob`, `3` with `carol`), `alice` receives `8` Available Shares, `$8.00` Price Improvement refunds to `alice`'s Available Cash, `alice`'s order is `FILLED`, and `carol` has `7` shares resting.
- **AC-03** (AF-01, SRS/BR-03): Given `alice` submits a Limit Buy for `10.5` shares or at `$0.00` or for `10,001` shares, then the order is rejected and `alice`'s balances do not change.
- **AC-04** (AF-02, SRS/BR-10): Given `alice` has `$1,000.00` Available Cash and `0` `AAPL` shares, when `alice` submits a Limit Buy for `10` `AAPL` at `$220.00` OR a Limit Sell for `1` `AAPL` at `$220.00`, then the order is `REJECTED` (no short-selling or overdraft).
- **AC-05** (AF-03, SRS/BR-07): Given `alice` has a resting Sell of `10` `AAPL` at `$220.00` and `bob` has a resting Sell of `10` `AAPL` at `$220.50`, when `alice` submits a Limit Buy for `5` `AAPL` at `$221.00`, then `alice`'s resting Sell is auto-cancelled (returning `10` shares to `alice`'s Available Shares) and `alice` buys `5` shares from `bob` at `$220.50`.
- **AC-06** (AF-04, SRS/BR-16): Given `alice` already has `50` active resting orders, when `alice` submits a 51st Limit Order, then the request is rejected.
- **AC-07** (AF-05): Given `alice` submits a Limit Buy with idempotency key `key-123` and receives an acknowledgment, when the exact request with `key-123` is retried, then no second order is created and the original acknowledgment is returned.

## Out of scope
- Time-in-force flags (IOC, FOK, GTD) and Stop/Stop-Limit orders.
