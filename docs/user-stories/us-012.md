# US-012: Maintain continuous liquidity via Market Bots

## Story
**As a** Market Bot
**I want** to autonomously place and cancel Limit and Market orders across all seeded Assets using Market Maker, Noise, Trend-Follower, and Mean-Reversion strategies
**so that** every Asset maintains an active two-sided Order Book and believable continuous price discovery 24/7.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Exchange is seeded with finite Market Bot Accounts (`is_bot = true`) holding initial cash and share inventories.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Market Bot authenticates as a first-class Account and observes live Order Book depth, Last Traded Price (or Initial Reference Price), and recent Trades for each seeded Asset.
- **BF-02:** Market Maker bots quote resting Bids and Asks around the reference/last price and skew quotes based on their current share inventory; Noise, Trend-Follower, and Mean-Reversion bots submit and cancel orders according to their strategies.
- **BF-03:** System validates, matches, reserves, and settles every Market Bot order using the exact same Matching Engine and accounting rules as human Traders.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-03): If a Market Bot attempts to buy without enough Available Cash or sell without enough Available Shares, then System marks the order `REJECTED` just like a human Trader (no negative balances or short-selling). → End.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-02, SRS/OBJ-01): Given the exchange runs unattended with no human Traders, over any 5-minute window each of the 5 seeded Assets has at least `1` executed Trade and non-empty Bid and Ask sides.
- **AC-02** (BF-03, AF-01, SRS/BR-10, SRS/BR-14): Given a Market Bot holds `0` Available Shares of `AAPL`, when it submits a Sell order for `10` `AAPL`, then System marks the order `REJECTED` and the bot's balance stays `0`.
- **AC-03** (BF-03, SRS/BR-11, SRS/BR-14): Given human Traders and Market Bots trade concurrently for 1,000+ operations, when a reconciliation runs across all Accounts (Traders, Market Bots, and Exchange Treasury), then total cash drift is `0` cents and total share drift is `0` shares per Asset.

## Out of scope
- AI/LLM-driven Market Bots (deferred to V2).
