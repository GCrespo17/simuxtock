# US-011: Reset Trader account

## Story
**As a** Trader
**I want** to reset my account back to `$10,000.00` in simulated cash once every 24 hours
**so that** I can start fresh if I lose my balance without distorting market prices.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Trader is signed in and has not reset their account within the past 24 hours.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Trader triggers an account reset.
- **BF-02:** System verifies the 24-hour cooldown has elapsed and the Exchange Treasury has at least `$10,000.00` available.
- **BF-03:** System auto-cancels all of the Trader's active resting orders across all Assets.
- **BF-04:** System transfers all of the Trader's remaining cash and shares off-book back to the Exchange Treasury (without executing against any Order Book), resets Realized PnL and Average Cost Basis to `$0.00`, transfers `$10,000.00` from the Exchange Treasury to the Trader's Available Cash, and records the reset timestamp.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-02): If fewer than 24 hours have passed since the Trader's last reset, then System rejects the reset and indicates when the cooldown expires. → End.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-03, BF-04, SRS/BR-11, SRS/BR-15): Given `alice` holds `$2,000.00` Available Cash, `$1,000.00` Reserved Cash in an `OPEN` Buy order, `15` `AAPL` shares, and `-$500.00` Realized PnL, when `alice` resets her account, then her `OPEN` order is `CANCELLED`, `$3,000.00` and `15` `AAPL` shares transfer to the Exchange Treasury off-book, `$10,000.00` transfers from the Exchange Treasury to `alice`, her Realized PnL and cost basis reset to `$0.00`, `AAPL`'s Last Traded Price is unchanged, and total system cash and `AAPL` shares remain strictly conserved.
- **AC-02** (AF-01, SRS/BR-15): Given `alice` reset her account `6` hours ago, when `alice` attempts to reset again, then the request is rejected and her balances and orders are unchanged.

## Out of scope
- Paid or instant cooldown bypasses.
