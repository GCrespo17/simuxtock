# US-007: Place a Market Order

## Story
**As a** Trader
**I want** to place Market Buy and Market Sell orders
**so that** I can execute immediately against the best available resting liquidity without setting a limit price.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Trader is signed in and selects a valid seeded Asset.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Trader submits a Market Order specifying Asset, side (`BUY` or `SELL`), and whole-share quantity.
- **BF-02:** System validates quantity (`1` to `10,000`) and rate limits; for a Market Sell, System verifies and reserves `qty` Available Shares.
- **BF-03:** System assigns the next per-Asset Sequence Number and sweeps opposite-side Price Levels in strict Price-Time Priority (FIFO), executing at each Maker's price.
- **BF-04:** System settles each Trade immediately (Market Buys draw directly from Available Cash on each fill) and marks the order `FILLED` when `remaining_quantity == 0`.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-03): If opposite-side liquidity is empty or exhausted before the full quantity fills, then System auto-cancels the unfilled remainder (`CANCELLED`), releases any remaining Seller share reservation, and never rests the Market Order on the Order Book. → End.
- **AF-02** (from BF-03): If a Market Buyer runs out of Available Cash to afford even `1` share at the next Ask Price Level, then System keeps all prior fills and auto-cancels the unfilled remainder (`CANCELLED`). → End.
- **AF-03** (from BF-02): If a Market Seller lacks `qty` Available Shares, or quantity is invalid (`< 1`, fractional, or `> 10,000`), then System marks the order `REJECTED`. → End.
- **AF-04** (from BF-03): If a Market Order encounters the same Account's resting order, then System auto-cancels the resting order (Self-Trade Prevention) and continues sweeping. → Resume at BF-03.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-03, BF-04, SRS/BR-05, SRS/BR-06): Given resting Asks of `4` shares at `$100.00` and `6` shares at `$101.00`, when `alice` (with `$2,000.00` Available Cash) submits a Market Buy for `7` shares, then `alice` executes `4` shares at `$100.00` and `3` shares at `$101.00`, pays `$703.00` total, Last Traded Price becomes `$101.00`, and the order is `FILLED`.
- **AC-02** (AF-01, SRS/BR-09, SRS/BR-10): Given only `3` resting Bids at `$100.00`, when `bob` (holding `10` Available Shares) submits a Market Sell for `10` shares, then `3` shares fill at `$100.00`, the remaining `7` shares are auto-cancelled and returned to `bob`'s Available Shares, the order ends `CANCELLED` with `filled_quantity: 3`, and nothing rests on the Ask side.
- **AC-03** (AF-02, SRS/BR-09, SRS/BR-10): Given `alice` has `$250.00` Available Cash and resting Asks of `2` shares at `$100.00` and `5` shares at `$101.00`, when `alice` submits a Market Buy for `5` shares, then `alice` buys `2` shares at `$100.00` (leaving `$50.00` cash), cannot afford a share at `$101.00`, and the remaining `3` shares auto-cancel.
- **AC-04** (AF-03, SRS/BR-03, SRS/BR-10): Given `bob` holds `2` Available Shares, when `bob` submits a Market Sell for `5` shares, then the order is `REJECTED` and `bob` retains `2` Available Shares.
- **AC-05** (AF-04, SRS/BR-07): Given `alice` has a resting Ask of `5` shares at `$100.00` and `bob` has a resting Ask of `5` shares at `$100.50`, when `alice` submits a Market Buy for `3` shares, then `alice`'s resting Ask is auto-cancelled and `alice` buys `3` shares from `bob` at `$100.50`.

## Out of scope
- Slippage protection caps on Market Orders.
