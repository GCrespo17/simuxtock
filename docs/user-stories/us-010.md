# US-010: View real-time portfolio and personal history

## Story
**As a** Trader
**I want** to view my real-time balances, holdings, cost basis, PnL, and personal order and trade history
**so that** I can evaluate my trading performance and verify every fill.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Trader is signed in.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Trader opens their portfolio and sees Available Cash, Reserved Cash, per-Asset Available/Reserved Shares, weighted Average Cost Basis, Holdings Value, Realized PnL, Unrealized PnL, Total Equity, and Total Return %.
- **BF-02:** Whenever the Trader's order status changes, a Trade executes, or an Asset's Last Traded Price moves, System pushes live private updates to the Trader's balances, active orders, and PnL.
- **BF-03:** Trader views their personal order history (filterable by Asset and lifecycle state) and personal executed Trade history.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-01, SRS/BR-12): Given `alice` starts with `$10,000.00`, buys `10` `AAPL` at `$100.00`, then buys `10` `AAPL` at `$120.00`, and `AAPL`'s Last Traded Price is `$130.00`, when `alice` views her portfolio, then she sees `$7,800.00` Available Cash, `20` Available Shares, Average Cost Basis `$110.00`, Holdings Value `$2,600.00`, Realized PnL `$0.00`, Unrealized PnL `+$400.00`, Total Equity `$10,400.00`, and Total Return `+4.00%`.
- **AC-02** (BF-01, SRS/BR-12): Continuing from `AC-01`, when `alice` sells `5` `AAPL` at `$130.00`, then Realized PnL permanently increases to `+$100.00`, Average Cost Basis stays `$110.00` for her remaining `15` shares, Unrealized PnL is `+$300.00`, and `Total Equity ($10,400.00) - Starting Cash ($10,000.00) == Realized ($100.00) + Unrealized ($300.00)`.
- **AC-03** (BF-01, SRS/BR-12): Continuing from `AC-02`, when `alice` sells her remaining `15` `AAPL` shares, then her `AAPL` Average Cost Basis resets to `$0.00` and Unrealized PnL for `AAPL` becomes `$0.00`.
- **AC-04** (BF-02): Given `alice` has a resting Limit Buy that partially fills for `3` shares while she is viewing the app, then her Available Shares, Reserved Cash, and active order status update automatically without a page refresh.
- **AC-05** (BF-03): Given `alice` has orders across `AAPL` and `NVDA` in `OPEN`, `FILLED`, and `CANCELLED` states, when `alice` filters order history by `AAPL` and `FILLED`, then only her `FILLED` `AAPL` orders appear (and never another Trader's orders).

## Out of scope
- Tax-lot accounting (FIFO/LIFO tax lots) and CSV portfolio exports.
