# US-002: Register a Trader account

## Story
**As a** Guest
**I want** to register a new Trader account
**so that** I receive `$10,000.00` in simulated cash and can start trading.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Exchange is seeded and the Exchange Treasury has at least `$10,000.00` in Available Cash.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Guest submits unique registration credentials (username/email and password).
- **BF-02:** System validates credential uniqueness and password rules.
- **BF-03:** System creates the Trader Account, transfers `$10,000.00` from the Exchange Treasury to the Trader's Available Cash, and signs the Trader in.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-02): If the submitted username or email is already registered, then System rejects registration without creating an Account or modifying Treasury balances. → End.
- **AF-02** (from BF-02): If the credentials fail validation rules (e.g., empty username or weak password), then System rejects registration with a validation error. → End.
- **AF-03** (from BF-03): If the Exchange Treasury has less than `$10,000.00` available, then System rejects registration without minting cash off-ledger. → End.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-03, SRS/BR-11, SRS/BR-15): Given the Exchange Treasury holds `$100,000,000.00`, when a Guest registers as `alice`, then `alice` receives `$10,000.00` Available Cash (`$0.00` Reserved), the Exchange Treasury decreases to `$99,990,000.00`, and total system cash remains unchanged.
- **AC-02** (AF-01): Given `alice` is already registered, when another Guest registers with `alice`, then registration is rejected and the Exchange Treasury balance is unchanged.
- **AC-03** (AF-02): Given a Guest submits an invalid password, when they submit registration, then registration is rejected with a validation message.
- **AC-04** (AF-03, SRS/BR-11): Given the Exchange Treasury holds `$5,000.00`, when a Guest attempts to register, then registration is rejected and no cash is created.

## Out of scope
- Email verification, OAuth/social login, and real-money deposits.

## Open questions
- None.
