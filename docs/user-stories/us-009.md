# US-009: Modify an active Limit Order

## Story
**As a** Trader
**I want** to modify the price or quantity of an active Limit Order
**so that** I can adjust my quote without manually cancelling and re-entering from scratch.

## Pre-conditions
- **PRE-01:** Trader is signed in and owns an active (`OPEN` or `PARTIALLY_FILLED`) Limit Order.

## Basic flow
- **BF-01:** Trader submits a modification specifying a new price and/or new whole-share quantity for an active Limit Order.
- **BF-02:** System assigns the next per-Asset Sequence Number and atomically executes a Cancel of the existing order (releasing its unfilled reservation) followed by a New Limit Order with the updated parameters.
- **BF-03:** The replacement order enters matching (if crossing) or rests at the back of the FIFO queue at its new Price Level, forfeiting the original order's time priority.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01** (from BF-02): If the modified parameters are invalid (e.g., bad tick or quantity `> 10,000`) or the Trader lacks sufficient Available balance even after releasing the old order's reservation, then System rejects the modification and leaves the original order untouched on the Order Book. → End.
- **AF-02** (from BF-02): If the original order is no longer active or belongs to another Account, then System rejects the modification. → End.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BF-02, BF-03, SRS/BR-09, SRS/BR-10): Given `alice` has an `OPEN` Limit Buy for `10` `AAPL` at `$200.00` (`$2,000.00` Reserved) and `$500.00` Available Cash, when `alice` modifies the order to `10` shares at `$210.00`, then the old order is `CANCELLED`, a new order for `10` at `$210.00` reserves `$2,100.00` (leaving `$400.00` Available), and it is placed behind any earlier resting orders at `$210.00`.
- **AC-02** (BF-03, SRS/BR-05, SRS/BR-09): Given `alice` is first in line at `$200.00` ahead of `bob` at `$200.00`, when `alice` modifies her quantity from `10` to `15` at `$200.00`, then `alice` forfeits time priority and queues behind `bob`.
- **AC-03** (AF-01): Given `alice` has `$100.00` Available Cash and an `OPEN` Limit Buy for `10` at `$200.00`, when `alice` attempts to modify it to `10` at `$250.00` (needing `$2,500.00` total, short by `$400.00`), then the modification is rejected and her original `10` at `$200.00` order remains `OPEN` with its original time priority.
- **AC-04** (AF-02): Given `bob` attempts to modify `alice`'s active order, then System rejects the request.

## Out of scope
- In-place quantity reduction that preserves queue priority (V1 strictly models all modifications as Cancel + New Order).
