# US-005: View live OHLCV candlestick charts

## Story
**As a** Guest or Trader
**I want** to view live-updating OHLCV candlestick charts across `1m`, `5m`, and `1h` timeframes
**so that** I can track price history and momentum for any Asset.

## Business rules
- **BR-01:** Charts default to the `1m` timeframe and allow switching between `1m`, `5m`, and `1h`.

## Alternate flows
- **AF-01:** If zero Trades occur for an Asset during a completed interval, then System emits a flat carry-forward bar (`O = H = L = C = previous_close`, `V = 0`).

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** (BR-01, SRS/BR-13): Given `AAPL` has three Trades in the `10:01` `1m` interval—`10` shares at `$220.00`, `5` shares at `$221.50`, and `20` shares at `$219.80`—when a Guest views the default `1m` chart, then the `10:01` candle shows `O: $220.00`, `H: $221.50`, `L: $219.80`, `C: $219.80`, `V: 35`.
- **AC-02** (BR-01): Given a Guest is viewing the `1m` chart for `AAPL`, when they switch to `5m` or `1h`, then the chart displays candles aggregated over `5m` or `1h` intervals respectively.
- **AC-03** (AF-01, SRS/BR-13): Given the `10:01` `1m` candle closed at `$219.80` and zero Trades occur between `10:02:00` and `10:02:59`, when the `10:02` interval completes, then the `10:02` candle is `O: $219.80, H: $219.80, L: $219.80, C: $219.80, V: 0`.
- **AC-04** (SRS/BR-13): Given a new Trade executes at `$220.40` for `15` shares during the active `10:03` candle, when the update streams to the viewer, then the active candle updates its High, Close, and Volume in real time without altering prior closed candles.

## Out of scope
- Technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands) and drawing tools.
